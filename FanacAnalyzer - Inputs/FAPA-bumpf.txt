FAPA was the first amateur press association devoted to SF fandom. Started in 1937 by Donald A. Wollheim, it is still running today (November, 2022). Over the years it has been a venue of choice for a variety of fandom's best-known fans. Many of the most famous fanzines appeared in FAPA. This listing will provide links to the zines we have online that were distributed in each FAPA mailing. As we add more scans, the list will be updated to include them. For more info about FAPA, see https://fancyclopedia.org/FAPA
