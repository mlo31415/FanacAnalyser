APA-F - was the first weekly (and local) fannish amateur press association. The largely overlapping membership of two 1960s New York City fan clubs, Fanoclasts and FIStFA, which met on alternating Fridays, served as the apa's distribution sites. The F was a nod to the clubs, but APA-F is also FAPA spelled backward. For more info, see https://fancyclopedia.org/APA-F
